### Does it meet MAX_DAYS_SUBMIT_TO_ACCEPT = 72?
**Marginal.** On the 12-month acceptance window the median is 71 d (meets); on the 24-month publication window it is 74 d (fails by 2 days). The 12-month "accepted" window is also biased toward short durations (papers received recently and still under review are not yet visible). The honest statement is therefore: **the median is ≈ 71–74 days, i.e. at the threshold; roughly half of papers are accepted within 72 days.** No other verifiable reputable candidate came close (next best: Scientific Reports, 123–132 d). Per Section 4 ("if none meets every rule, pick the closest legitimate Q1 journal and state exactly which rule it fails"), JKSU-CIS is selected with this caveat stated.
