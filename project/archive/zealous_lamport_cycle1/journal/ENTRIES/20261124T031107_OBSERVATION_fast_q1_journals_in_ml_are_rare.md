# OBSERVATION: Fast Q1 journals in ML are rare

*Written 2026-11-24T03:11:07+00:00*

I sampled publisher-deposited Received/Accepted dates for 18 reputable AI/CS journals via Crossref. Almost all have medians of 4–9 months (e.g. Neural Computing and Applications 254.5 d, Journal of Big Data 201.5 d, Scientific Reports 132 d; journal_selection/turnaround_summary.csv). Only JKSU-CIS sits at the 72-day line (median 74 d on a 24-month window, 71 d on last-12-month acceptances). The fast Elsevier journals I suspected might qualify could not be verified because their dates are not in Crossref and ScienceDirect blocks me, so I excluded them rather than guess. The turnaround constraint, not scientific fit, decided the venue.
