# OBSERVATION: The weight I use is Chapelle's, and the PU reference is fabricated

*Written 2026-11-24T23:21:09+00:00*

The literature batch (37 papers opened, 21 full text; literature/prior_art_batch2.json) found no JIT-SDP work that models the probability that a provisionally clean commit is defective as a function of its age. But the weighting q = pS/(1-p+pS) is in substance Chapelle's delayed-feedback model (KDD 2014) for not-yet-converted clicks. I must credit it and cannot claim the weight. What remains is narrower: a right-truncation-corrected delay estimate (defects are only seen once found, unlike clicks, whose whole cohort is observed) and its use in online JIT-SDP. Falessi et al. (TOSEM 2021) handle dormant defects by dropping recent classes per release, a hard rule. The Liu 2024a reference cited by MSTDP points to article numbers that do not exist in EMSE 29(5), and its neighbour (Liu 2024b, 29(3):51) fails the same way. I conclude MSTDP's reference list contains invented entries. Combined with the dev results so far, novelty of LAW as a method is weak (differentiation maybe 2/5); the operating-point finding (H2) may turn out to be the stronger contribution.
