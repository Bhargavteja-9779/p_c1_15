# Highlights

- Waiting 90 days removes only a median 15.3% of false-clean labels in online JIT-SDP.
- True labels on the waited-out data recover 108% and 90% of the wait–oracle gap.
- The verification-latency penalty is mainly label noise from old dormant defects.
- Age-only corrections fail; feature-based denoisers help partly but not significantly.
- Label-policy comparisons are confounded by the decision threshold; report AUC too.
