# Highlights

- Waiting 90 days removes only a median 15.3% of false-clean training labels.
- At a 30-day wait, true labels close 0.81 and 0.92 of the AUC gap to an oracle.
- Harmful labels sit on commits 30–180 days old; older commits add nothing.
- Age-only and feature-based corrections do not beat a 90-day wait on fresh data.
- A latency-aware threshold helps only label policies with a distorted class prior.
